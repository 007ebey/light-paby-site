What We Do

Marriage Counselling

Together, we walk with couples seeking restoration and direction. Through Spirit-led counsel, we help rebuild trust, restore intimacy, and realign marriages with God’s purpose.

About Us

Welcome to our heart, ministry, and calling—I’m Aby P Rajan, Lead Pastor at HisNearness Church, Bengaluru. Alongside my wife, Pastor Smitha, we serve God’s people with a passion for His presence and purpose.

Our journey is rooted in a deep hunger for Jesus. Together, we lead, mentor, and build lives through prayer, teaching, and prophetic counsel. Our heart is to reveal Jesus, restore lives, and release people into their Kingdom identity.

To see individuals and families transformed by the presence of God, walking in healing, maturity, and purpose.
We long to see revival in hearts, homes, and nations—where Jesus is exalted, lives are restored, and the Church rises in glory.
